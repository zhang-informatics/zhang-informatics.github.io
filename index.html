Zhang’s Lab

Zhang's Lab at the University of Minnesota focuses on biomedical informatics, AI in healthcare, and computational methods to advance precision medicine. Our mission is to translate cutting-edge informatics research into clinical applications that improve healthcare outcomes. Our team works at the intersection of clinical data, natural language processing, knowledge graphs, and AI-driven decision support.

About Dr. Rui Zhang

Dr. Rui Zhang is a Professor in the Department of Surgery and Director of the Biomedical Health Informatics Lab at the University of Minnesota. His research focuses on AI in healthcare, clinical natural language processing, biomedical knowledge graph construction, and health informatics tools for precision medicine. He has led multiple federally funded research projects and published extensively in top informatics and medical journals.
